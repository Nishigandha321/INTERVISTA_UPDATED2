HOW INTERVISTA WORKS

From Practice to Progress

Each interview turns into a clearer picture of what to practise next.

  1. 01

    Candidate State

    Current skill estimates and identified weaknesses.

  2. 02

    Take Interview

    Assess your skills through the relevant interview module.

  3. 03

    Identify Weaknesses

    Find the skills or topics that need more attention.

  4. 04

    Get Learning Resources

    Receive learning material matched to your weak areas.

  5. 05

    Learn and Practise

    Study the resources and practise the areas you want to strengthen.

  6. 06

    Retry Interview

    Reassess performance after learning and practice.

  7. 07

    Update Candidate State

    Refresh skill estimates and performance from the new assessment.

  8. Repeat

    The updated candidate state guides the next cycle.
